What this is

The gap between what students are taught about communications and what the profession looks like on the ground is wide, and it is getting wider.

Tech & Comms is The Comms Boardroom's attempt to close that gap early, while students still have time to act on it. It is not a lecture series. It is a set of straight conversations, led by working practitioners, built around three things every Mass Communication student should hear before they graduate.

Most curricula were designed for a version of the industry that no longer fully exists. Students leave fluent in theory but with little exposure to how communications works inside a real organisation, how much technology now shapes the work, or how they are expected to present themselves professionally. The information exists. It usually arrives after graduation. We would rather bring it while students are still curious and still have room to experiment.

Graduates who are ready for the profession as it is.

A generation of Nigerian communications graduates who walk into their first role already fluent in how PR, data and technology work together.
